Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

ChipIn is an app for splitting shared costs with the people you actually share them with. This policy explains what it stores, why, and how to get rid of it. It covers the ChipIn iOS app and its backend.

What ChipIn collects

Your account

Email addressUsed to sign in, confirm your account and reset your password.
Display nameShown to other members of groups you join.
Phone numberOptional. Only stored if you enter it.
Colour themeSo your chosen appearance follows you across devices.

Your groups and spending

Group detailsName, type, currency, and a photo if you add one.
ExpensesAmount, description, date, who paid, and how it was split.
SettlementsRecords of payments between members, to keep balances correct.

Everything in a group is visible to the other members of that group, and to nobody else. Expense descriptions are free text — please don't put anything in them you wouldn't want your group to read.

Your device

Push tokenOnly if you allow notifications. Used to tell you when someone adds an expense.

ChipIn has no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party profiling. Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication, and group photos.
  • Expo push notification service — delivers notifications; receives your device token and the notification text.
  • Apple — distributes the app via TestFlight and the App Store.

These providers process data to run the service, not for their own purposes.

How it's protected

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Database access is enforced per-row: you can only read data for groups you belong to.
  • Group photos are kept in private storage and served through short-lived signed links, so they aren't publicly reachable.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account. No email required.

What is deleted: your name, email, phone, profile, group memberships, and notification tokens. Your login stops working immediately. Groups left with no members are deleted entirely, along with their history.

What is kept: expenses you were part of stay in their groups, attributed to “Deleted user”. This is deliberate — other members' balances are calculated from those expenses, so deleting them would make everyone else's totals wrong. The retained records contain no personal details about you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to complain to a data protection authority. Deletion and correction are built into the app; for anything else, get in touch.

Children

ChipIn isn't directed at children under 13, and isn't knowingly used by them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be announced in the app.
